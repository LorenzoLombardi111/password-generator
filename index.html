Is freepasswordgenerator.app really free?

Are the passwords generated truly random?

Yes, when done correctly. freepasswordgenerator.app generates all passwords entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. No passwords are ever sent to any server. The tool works offline once loaded and uses no analytics or tracking scripts.

What is a passphrase and should I use one?

A passphrase is a password made of multiple random words, like "correct-horse-battery-staple". Passphrases are easier to remember while still being very secure. A 4-word passphrase from a 2048-word list provides about 44 bits of entropy, and longer passphrases provide even more security.

How long should my password be?

For most accounts, 16 characters or more is recommended. For high-security accounts like email or banking, use 20+ characters. freepasswordgenerator.app supports passwords up to 128 characters long. The longer the password and the more character types included, the stronger it will be.

Does freepasswordgenerator.app store my passwords?

No. freepasswordgenerator.app never stores passwords in localStorage or on any server. A temporary history of your last 20 generated passwords is kept in sessionStorage, which is automatically cleared when you close the browser tab. No data ever leaves your browser.
